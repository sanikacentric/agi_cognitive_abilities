⚠ The Core Finding

When GPT-4o answers a question incorrectly, it expresses ≥70% confidence 80% of the time. This "confident wrongness" is completely invisible to standard accuracy benchmarks. A model that scores 58% accuracy can look fine — while failing catastrophically on the metric that matters most for safe deployment: does it know what it doesn't know?
